# Crosses the spread: fills order 2, then order 3 (time priority), rests 50.
new,AAPL,6,buy,limit,10055,350,6
# A smaller size keeps time priority; a new price loses it.
modify,AAPL,4,,,10045,250

# A market sell sweeps the bids, best price first.
new,AAPL,7,sell,market,,600,7
# IOC: takes what it can at or below its limit, cancels the rest.

# A fill-or-kill that cannot fill completely is killed; the book is untouched.
